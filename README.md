HomeKeep is a household expiration and reminder management platform that helps families keep track of important dates for everyday items and services. Family members can maintain a shared list of warranties, insurance policies, subscriptions, documents, product shelf lives, and maintenance schedules, with the system automatically calculating remaining days and highlighting upcoming or expired items. The platform also supports recurring reminders, categorization, notifications, and a centralized dashboard, providing families with a simple way to manage important household deadlines in one place.
